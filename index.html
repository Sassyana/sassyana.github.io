How It All Started

It started with a simple rule: stop posting boring content.

Sassy Ana Studios was born out of a realization that most business content online gets completely ignored. Traditional ads feel forced, static posts get scrolled past, and small business owners rarely have the time or team to produce original short-form video every single week.

By combining cutting-edge AI animation and puppet rigging tools with original storytelling and witty creative scripts, I developed a workflow that turns simple ideas, photos, and product listings into entertaining, scroll-stopping videos.

My vision is straightforward: give local Canadian businesses access to agency-level video creativity—without the giant agency price tag. Whether it's a dynamic real estate walkthrough or a customized AI brand character that gives your business an instant voice, we create content that people actually enjoy watching.
